Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. Here is exactly what Clipr does and does not send.

Nothing uploaded

Your clips and videos stay on your computer.

No account

Nothing to sign up for, and no ads.

Reports only if you send one

Nothing goes out unless you press Send report.

Everything runs on your computer

Clipr is a desktop app. Cutting, downloading and joining all happen on your machine, and the clips go straight to the folder you chose. Nothing you watch, mark or make is uploaded to us.

No account, no ads, no tracking

You do not make an account to use Clipr. There are no ads in the app or on this site.

Signing in to YouTube

YouTube serves 360p to anyone who is not signed in. For 1080p, Clipr asks once for a cookies.txt exported from your own browser, and walks you through it. That file stays on your computer and is never uploaded. You can skip it and work at 360p.

Problem reports

If something goes wrong, you can choose Help then Report a problem, write what you were doing in plain words and press Send report. Clipr adds the error, the version numbers and a cleaned-up log. Nothing is sent unless you press Send report, and you do not need an account of any kind. A crash screen has the same button.

Updates and this site

Clipr checks for new versions of itself, and the download and what's new pages on this site read the public list of releases to show the latest version number and date. Neither sends anything about you or your clips.

Other services

To get a video, Clipr talks to the video service you pasted a link from, from your own computer, like a browser would. That service's own terms and privacy policy apply to that part.
